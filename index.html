Support

Frequently
asked

Can't find what you need? The KEMET assistant answers instantly, or write to us at hello@kemet-fashion.com.

Where do you deliver?

KEMET is an online label available across Egypt. We deliver to every governorate in 2–4 working days, with free shipping on orders over 2,000 EGP.

How do I choose my size?

Our tees, hoodies and shirts are cut with a relaxed, slightly oversized shoulder. If you are between sizes, take the smaller one for a cleaner line, or your usual size for the intended fit.

Can I return or exchange a piece?

Yes — returns and exchanges are accepted within 14 days of delivery, as long as the piece is unworn with its tags attached.

How should I care for the fabrics?

Cold wash inside out, dry in shade, and avoid direct heat on printed panels. Linen pieces soften with every wash.

How do I pay?

Card payments and cash on delivery are both available everywhere we ship in Egypt.
